About research

Overview

This research proposes an integrated AI-based system for water quality and contamination assessment. It brings together four components developed by different team members: visual contamination detection, physicochemical water quality classification, microorganism detection, and an AI-based water safety report and purification advisor.

Together, the components assess water samples and, where necessary, recommend corrective action.

Scope
  • Detect and localize visible contaminants in water sample photographs.
  • Classify water quality from physicochemical measurements.
  • Identify and localize microorganisms from microscope imagery.
  • Combine component results into a water safety assessment.
  • Recommend purification procedures for unsafe water using retrieved guidance.
  • Calculate chemical dosages through deterministic, rule-based calculations.
Four components

One system, four parts

1Component 1

Contamination Detection

Water sample photographs in, bounding boxes with contamination type and severity out.

2Component 2

Water Quality Classification

Measured water parameters in, a safe or unsafe result with parameter-level flags out.

3Component 3

Microorganism Detection

Microscope images in, bounding boxes with the detected species or genus out.

4Component 4

Water Safety Report & Purification Advisor

Results of components 1 to 3 in, a diagnosis summary, purification steps and calculated dosages out.
