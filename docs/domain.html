Research gap

What is missing today

This research seeks to address the need for an integrated assessment workflow that combines image-based contamination detection, physicochemical classification, microscopy-based microorganism detection, and purification advice.

This gap will be supported by the literature survey above.

Research problem

The problem and the proposed solution

Problem statement

Water quality evaluation involves several kinds of evidence, including visible contaminants, measured physicochemical parameters, and microorganisms. A unified assessment and actionable purification guidance are needed to bring these findings together.

Proposed solution

The proposed system receives water sample photographs, physicochemical measurements and microscope images. Components 1 to 3 analyze these inputs. Component 4 combines their outputs, generates a diagnosis summary, and provides purification instructions and rule-calculated chemical dosages where needed.

Research objectives

What the team set out to achieve

Main objective

Develop an integrated AI-based system to assess water quality and contamination and provide appropriate purification guidance.

Component objectives and novelty

1Component 1

Contamination Detection

Member: WIJESINGHA WASPD

Objective. Identify and localize visible contaminants in water images using deep learning object detection.

Novelty. A water-specific detection approach that combines contamination localisation with image-quality checks and validated severity estimation, helping distinguish contamination from poor lighting or unclear images.

2Component 2

Water Quality Classification

Member: DE SILVA WNN

Objective. Classify water quality from physicochemical parameters using supervised machine learning.

Novelty. A classification approach that combines machine-learning predictions with parameter-level threshold checks and missing-data warnings, providing an explainable assessment and highlighting disagreements between predictions and measured parameters.

3Component 3

Microorganism Detection

Member: DODANWELA D

Objective. Detect and localize microorganisms in microscope images using deep learning object detection.

Novelty. A microscopy-based approach that combines small-organism detection, counting and uncertainty handling, referring ambiguous images for expert review and reporting only categories supported by validated reference labels.

4Component 4

Water Safety Report & Purification Advisor

Member: RATHNAYAKE RMNA

Objective. Combine analysis results and generate a water safety report and contamination-specific purification guidance using RAG and deterministic dosage calculation.

Novelty. A RAG-based advisor that combines all three analytical outputs, checks whether guidance applies to the sample, and generates evidence-linked recommendations. Any dosage calculation uses separately validated rules rather than language-model estimates.

System architecture
1Component 1

Contamination Detection

Technology
Deep learning: computer vision and object detection
Proposed model
CNN-based object detector, such as YOLO or Faster R-CNN
Input
Water sample photographs
Targets
Turbidity, discoloration, oil sheen, algae bloom, floating debris and microplastics
Approach
Train an object detection model on labeled water sample photographs to identify and localize visible contamination.
Output
Bounding boxes, contamination type and severity
2Component 2

Water Quality Classification

Technology
Supervised machine learning
Proposed model
Random Forest or Gradient Boosting classifier
Input
Measured pH, hardness, turbidity, total dissolved solids (TDS) and fluoride level
Approach
Train a classifier on tabular water quality data to determine overall quality.
Output
Safe or unsafe classification, or a quality grade, with parameter-level flags
3Component 3

Microorganism Detection

Technology
Deep learning: object detection
Proposed model
CNN-based object detector, such as YOLO or Faster R-CNN
Input
Microscope imagery of water samples
Approach
Train on labeled microscopy datasets to identify and localize bacterial and other microorganism species.
Output
Bounding boxes with the detected species or genus
4Component 4

Water Safety Report & Purification Advisor

Technology
Generative AI: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Input
Outputs of components 1 to 3
Approach
Combine the detection and classification results. If the water is classified as safe, produce a concise confirmation report. If it is unsafe, retrieve contamination-specific purification guidance from a curated knowledge base and generate step-by-step instructions.
Knowledge base
WHO drinking-water guidelines and household water treatment references
Chemical calculator
Deterministic, rule-based calculation for quantities such as chlorination or coagulant dose. Dosage numbers are never invented by the language model.
Output
Diagnosis summary, purification steps and calculated dosages
